# AI Ad Engine

A system that writes, films, checks and tests video ads for e-commerce brands, then learns from the results.

- **Writes** scripts from real customer reviews with Claude.
- **Films** the scenes with Veo, Seedance and Kling.
- **Checks** every ad before it ships: lip sync, hands, product accuracy.
- **Runs** the ads on Meta, and each morning reads the results, pauses what isn't working, works out why, and writes new versions of the angles that sell.
- **Asks first:** anything that costs money waits for approval in Telegram; clients follow everything on a dashboard.

Used by six- and seven-figure brands to scale their creative output.

*Built with TypeScript, Next.js, Postgres, Claude, Gemini and video models. Source is private.*
