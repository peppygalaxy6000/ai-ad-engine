# Phantom

AI systems for creative and growth, built by Jesse Ifrah.

### AI ad engine
Writes, films, checks and tests video ads for e-commerce brands. Claude writes scripts from real customer reviews; Veo, Seedance and Kling film the scenes; every ad passes quality checks (lip sync, hands, product accuracy) before it goes live on Meta. Each morning the system reads the results, pauses what isn't working, works out why, and writes new versions of what sells. Used by six- and seven-figure brands to scale their creative output.

### Alfred
A personal AI chief of staff: a Telegram bot, a macOS menu-bar app and a Claude connector. Calendar, to-dos, research outreach with follow-ups, hackathon and internship scouting, meeting notes.

### Lynceus
An AI drone swarm that understands hand signs and voice. → [jesse-ifrah.pages.dev/articles/lynceus](https://jesse-ifrah.pages.dev/articles/lynceus)
